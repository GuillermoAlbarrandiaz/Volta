Home/About us

We look after homes in Barcelona

Volta is a company specialising in full property management, seasonal rentals, corporate rentals and real estate marketing in Barcelona.

Two activities, one team

Real estate marketing

We help owners market their properties and clients find homes to rent or buy.

Property management

We take care of ongoing management of the homes throughout the tenant's stay, with a personalised service for both the owner and the tenant. That's what sets us apart: instead of finishing our work when the contract is signed, we stay in charge of the home.

To do it well, we have an in-house maintenance team that lets us look after each home in a more coordinated, agile and personal way.

So the owner doesn't have to worry.

We believe that owning a rental home shouldn't be a second job. Our goal is that owners don't have to deal with day-to-day operations, while staying informed and deciding on what matters.

We prefer to explain things clearly, talk about real numbers and not promise what we can't deliver.

A relationship for the long term

We want to be every owner's trusted property partner. Our offer isn't just about finding a tenant, but about caring for, managing and supervising their property on an ongoing basis.
