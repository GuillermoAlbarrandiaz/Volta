New build with terrace and parking in Poblenou

Floor area87 m²
Bedrooms2
Bathrooms2

An 87 m² home in a new-build development in the heart of Poblenou, one of Barcelona's most attractive and up-and-coming neighbourhoods. It stands out for its contemporary design, high-quality finishes and a practical layout designed for maximum comfort.

It has two spacious double bedrooms and two full bathrooms, with comfortable, versatile spaces ideal for couples, families or professionals looking for a modern home in a prime location.

One of its main highlights is the private terrace, an outdoor space perfect for enjoying the Mediterranean climate. The home has underfloor heating and cooling, which regulates the temperature in winter and summer with even comfort and greater energy efficiency, and includes a parking space in the same building.

Poblenou combines its industrial past with an urban transformation that has made it a benchmark for modern living and quality of life, with restaurants, shops, sports centres, green spaces and good public transport. Its proximity to the 22@ district, the Rambla del Poblenou and the beaches makes it attractive both to live in and to invest in.
