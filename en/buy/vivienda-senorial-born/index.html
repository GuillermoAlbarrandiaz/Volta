Stately home facing the park

Floor area192 m²
Bedrooms3
Layout3 suites
TransactionSale

Stately home in an elegant classic building from 1888, next to El Born and facing Parc de la Ciutadella, one of Barcelona's most iconic green spaces.

It stands out for its character, space and light: high ceilings and seven balconies that bring in natural light and an exceptional connection with the surroundings, with open views over the park.

It is laid out as three spacious suites, giving each bedroom privacy and comfort, plus a separate study that can serve as a workspace, library or extra bedroom.

Surrounded by some of the city's most iconic cultural, culinary and architectural landmarks, a few minutes from the old town and with excellent connections to the rest of Barcelona.
