Home with terrace and parking in Vila de Gràcia

Floor area125 m²
Bedrooms3
Bathrooms2
TransactionSale

A 125 m² home in the heart of Vila de Gràcia, one of Barcelona's most iconic, authentic and sought-after neighbourhoods. In a building from 1935, it combines the charm of traditional Barcelona architecture with spacious, comfortable rooms.

It has three bedrooms and two full bathrooms, with a practical layout that makes the most of its floor area. Its generous size offers many options, both for families and for anyone who needs a study or extra workspace.

One of its main highlights is the private terrace overlooking a green area, a quiet, open outdoor space right in the centre of Barcelona. It also has a parking space in the same building, a real bonus in such a central, established neighbourhood.

Vila de Gràcia stands out for its lively streets, squares, traditional architecture and food and cultural scene, with markets, local shops, schools and all kinds of services. It offers easy access to Passeig de Gràcia, Avinguda Diagonal and Passeig de Sant Joan, with good public transport links.
