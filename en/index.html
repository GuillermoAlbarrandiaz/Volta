About us

We're a Barcelona team that cares for homes: we rent them out, sell them and, above all, manage them.

A real estate agency usually finishes its work when the contract is signed. That's where we start. We market your home and then stay in charge of it throughout the tenant's stay: we take their calls, coordinate maintenance and keep you informed.

How we work

We get to know your home

We visit the property, listen to what you need and propose the strategy and rental model that suit it best.
