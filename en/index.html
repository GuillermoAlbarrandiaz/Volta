Furnished apartments and property management in Barcelona.

Volta rents and manages homes in Barcelona: medium-term furnished apartments, corporate housing for companies, and full property management for owners who live elsewhere.

Get in touchSee our rentals

Looking for a home?

Medium-term rentals (over 31 days)

Furnished, fully equipped apartments for people spending a few months in Barcelona. Throughout your stay you deal with Volta, not the owner: we answer your calls and our own maintenance team fixes whatever comes up.

Corporate housing

We sign directly with companies that need to house their staff in Barcelona for a project, an onboarding or a relocation. Tell us your dates, number of people, preferred area and budget, and we will send you options.

We do not offer tourist or holiday lets.

Own a property in Barcelona?

Many of our owners live outside Barcelona or abroad. We market the property, choose the tenant with you, sign the contract and then stay in charge during the whole stay: tenant support, repairs with our in-house maintenance team, cleaning, check-in and check-out.

Since January 2026, Catalan law (Ley 11/2025) regulates seasonal rentals and applies rent caps in high-demand areas such as Barcelona. We help you choose the rental model that fits your property and set up the contract correctly.

We also help owners sell. We are members of API and AICAT, the professional real estate agent associations of Catalonia.
