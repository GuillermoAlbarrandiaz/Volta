Much more than renting out your property. We take care of everything.

We find the right tenant and manage everything that comes after. From day-to-day support to maintenance and resolving issues, our team looks after your property throughout the stay.

Your tenant calls us. You enjoy your property.

In a conventional rental, when the dishwasher breaks, there's a problem with the boiler or a question about utilities, the tenant calls the owner. And the owner has to take the call, find a technician and coordinate the repair.

With Volta, the tenant has our team as their point of contact. We take the request, handle the issue, coordinate the fix and check that everything works. You stay informed and decide on anything that needs your approval.

Frequently asked questions

What does full rental management in Barcelona include?

It includes marketing the home, choosing the tenant, the contract, move-in and, above all, management throughout the stay: tenant support, issues, maintenance by our own team, cleaning, check-in and check-out, and supervision of the home.

Who looks after the tenant when something breaks?

Volta does. The tenant contacts us directly and our team coordinates the fix. The owner only steps in when a decision needs their approval.

How much does full management cost?

It depends on the home and the rental model. We prepare a tailored proposal once we've seen the property, with no obligation.

Do you work all over Barcelona?

Yes. We manage homes across Barcelona's neighbourhoods, such as the Eixample, Gràcia, Sant Gervasi, Les Corts, Poblenou or Ciutat Vella.

Can I still make decisions about my home?

Yes. You hand over day-to-day management, but you stay informed and decide on everything important: price, tenant profile, significant repairs and renewals.
