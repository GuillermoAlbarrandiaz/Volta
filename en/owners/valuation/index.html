A decision based on data

The value of a home in Barcelona varies a lot from one neighbourhood to another, and choosing between selling and renting depends on more than price: the net rental yield after expenses and taxes, the owner's time horizon and the specific situation of the property.

That's why the estimate is only the starting point. We then review your case with you and explain, with no obligation, which option makes most sense: selling, renting or handing over management to Volta.
