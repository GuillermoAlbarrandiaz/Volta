Apartment with mezzanine in the Gothic Quarter

Floor area52 m²

Apartment in a historic building in the Barri Gòtic, a few minutes' walk from Plaça Catalunya.

The living area brings together the living room, kitchen and bathroom on the main floor, and the bedroom is on a mezzanine reached by a staircase. It keeps its wooden beams and has an exterior balcony.

Designed for one person or a couple who want to live in the centre, surrounded by shops, restaurants and good transport links.
