New-build apartment in Poblenou's 22@ district, in a building completed in 2022 with a rooftop communal pool and city views.

It has two bedrooms and two bathrooms, one of them en suite with the main bedroom. The kitchen is open to the living-dining room and the layout makes good use of natural light. It is exterior, on the first floor and comes fully furnished.

An area with parks, bike lanes, shops and good public transport, a few minutes from the beach. Designed for relocated professionals, couples or families looking for a medium-term stay.
